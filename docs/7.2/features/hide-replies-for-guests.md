# Hide the replies for guests, per category

> **Label:** `feature` · **Since:** Kunena 7.2 · **For:** Administrators · **Issue:** [#3104](https://github.com/Kunena/Kunena-Forum/issues/3104)

A category can hide the replies for guests, show them, or use the global option.

A new select **Hide replies for guests** in the category form (backend and frontend) gives each category its own choice: *Use the global
setting*, *Hide the replies* or *Show the replies*. Guests then must log in to read the replies of the category; the first message of a topic stays visible.

![The settings of a category](../images/admin-category-settings.png)

The choice is used for the messages, the preview of the last post, the search results and the feeds.
